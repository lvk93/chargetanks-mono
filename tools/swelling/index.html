🫁 Understand Swelling

🧠 What does this tool show?

Why can a battery get thicker? Three simple reasons:

  1. Breathing When SoC goes up or down, the anode and cathode gently expand and shrink (like lungs).
  2. Irreversible swelling Over life a thin SEI “crust” grows and adds permanent thickness (lower SoH → more swelling).
  3. Compression Squeezing the stack pushes back.
  • Cylindrical: the metal can fixes thickness; you can't tune the squeeze.
  • Pouch/Prismatic: you can clamp it (springs/pads). More squeeze = less empty space.

⚙️ Knobs

  • SoC drives breathing
  • SoH lower = more SEI swelling
  • Cylindrical toggle fixed-thickness can
  • Compression clamp force for pouch/prismatic cells
